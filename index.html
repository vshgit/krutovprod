How a project runs

The same stages as a film production. AI speeds up some of them, it doesn't replace them.

Idea

Brief, concept and script. Dmitry writes it.

Look

Characters, style frames and a storyboard you approve before production.

Production

Key frames, generation and, when needed, a shoot.

Post

Edit, sound design, music and colour.
